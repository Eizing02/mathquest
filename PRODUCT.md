# MathQuest Product Context

## Product
MathQuest is a Thai classroom attendance and rewards app for teachers and students. It helps teachers open attendance sessions, track class activity, manage points and shop items, grant free items, and export summaries. Students use it mostly on mobile to check in, see progress, and redeem rewards.

## Primary Users
- Teachers who need fast, reliable classroom operations during class time.
- Students who need a simple mobile experience for check-ins, rewards, and progress.
- School staff who review exported attendance and reward summaries.

## Core Jobs
- Start and manage attendance sessions with clear status.
- Search, filter, edit, and review students quickly.
- Manage rewards, shop items, orders, and free item grants.
- Export printable data without losing meaning.
- Give students a playful but readable progress experience.

## Personality
Helpful, school-friendly, encouraging, and clear. The UI can feel playful through rewards and progress feedback, but teacher workflows should stay calm, scannable, and dependable.

## Design Principles
- Task first, play as feedback.
- Mobile first for students, dense but clear for teachers.
- Every important state should be visible before the user acts.
- Use familiar controls and labels over novelty.
- Reports and exports must stay professional and printable.
- Keep data behavior stable; UI changes should not alter business logic.

## Anti-References
- Decorative dashboards that make teachers hunt for actions.
- Game-like visuals that reduce readability.
- Overly formal report styling inside day-to-day classroom tools.
- Hidden controls that require memorization.

## Accessibility Expectations
- High contrast text and controls.
- Visible keyboard focus.
- Comfortable touch targets on phones.
- Reduced motion support.
- Clear empty, loading, disabled, and error states.
